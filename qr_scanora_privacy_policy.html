QR Scanora

Privacy Policy · JK AppSivora

Effective date: 20 October 2026

1. Introduction

QR Scanora is a QR code and barcode scanning and creation application developed by JK AppSivora.

We value your privacy. QR Scanora is designed to provide its core features locally on your device without requiring you to create an account.

This Privacy Policy explains how the application accesses, uses, and stores information when you use its features.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the features you use, QR Scanora may access the following:

Camera: The camera is used to scan QR codes and supported barcodes. Camera access is used for scanning when you grant permission.

Photos and images: If you choose to scan a QR code from your gallery, the application accesses the image you select.

QR code content: The application processes content you scan or enter to create QR codes, such as website URLs, text, contact information, Wi-Fi details, and other supported content.

Local history and preferences: The application may store scan history, created QR codes, favorites, design preferences, and settings on your device.

Backup files: If you use Backup & Restore, the application may export or import supported records through files you choose to save or open.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the application is used to provide its features, including:

  • Scanning QR codes and barcodes.
  • Creating and customizing QR codes.
  • Saving and displaying scan history and favorites.
  • Managing templates and user preferences.
  • Exporting and restoring supported local records.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

QR Scanora is designed for local use. If the app operates as described and does not transmit user information to external services, scan records and created QR codes remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

We do not sell personal information.

When you choose to open a website, contact, email, phone number, or other external content from a QR code, the relevant application or service may process information according to its own privacy policy.

5. Internet and External Links

QR codes may contain external website links or other content. Opening those links can connect you to third-party services.

Please review the privacy policies of external websites and services before sharing personal information with them.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored records may remain on your device until you delete them using the application, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

Backup files exported to your device or another location may remain there until you delete them separately.

7. Data Security

We aim to minimize unnecessary data handling by supporting local processing and storage. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please protect your device and any exported backup files.

8. Children's Privacy

QR Scanora is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact the developer.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published at the privacy policy URL associated with the application.
